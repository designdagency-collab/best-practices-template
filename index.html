How it works

Three missions. One payday.

You've done the site visit, written the quote and won the job. Then the calendar filled up. Instead of ringing the customer to bail, sell the job to someone who can turn up.

1 Seller

List the job

Upload the quote, site photos and the install date the customer expects. Set your finder's fee. Most sellers ask 10% of the quoted value.

2 Buyer

A licensed installer buys it

Only ARCtick licence holders with a verified ABN can see customer details. They pay the fee, which we hold in escrow until the handover is confirmed.

3 Both

Hand over and get paid

You introduce the new installer to the customer with our handover template. Once the customer confirms, the fee lands in your account within 24 hours.
